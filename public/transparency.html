Transparency Seal

The Transparency Seal is a standard requirement under the annual General Appropriations Act (GAA), requiring national government agencies (and, by extension, similar bodies) to make key information publicly accessible on their websites — including their mandate, officials, budget, and annual procurement plan.

Citizen's Charter

A Citizen's Charter is a public document, required under the Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act (Republic Act No. 11032), that lists an office's frontline services, requirements, fees, and the maximum processing time for each — so the public knows exactly what to expect when transacting with a government office.

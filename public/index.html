Welcome to MAFAR-LDS

The Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Agrarian Reform – Lanao del Sur (MAFAR-LDS) is the office of the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao responsible for agricultural development, fisheries support, and agrarian reform services across the province. This portal is where the public can learn about our programs and leadership, and where our staff manage client and program records.

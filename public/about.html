About MAFAR-LDS

The Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Agrarian Reform (MAFAR) serves farmers, fisherfolk, and agrarian reform beneficiaries across the Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao (BARMM), including Lanao del Sur. This portal is MAFAR-LDS's internal system for managing client records and sectoral programs.

Vision, Mission and Mandate

The summary below reflects MAFAR's general mandate as a BARMM ministry. For the ministry's official, current vision/mission statement, please confirm with MAFAR's Public Affairs/Information Office.

Vision

A Bangsamoro where farmers, fisherfolk, and agrarian reform beneficiaries lead food-secure, resilient, and prosperous communities.

Mission

To develop and deliver agriculture, fisheries, and agrarian reform policies, programs, and services that raise productivity, protect natural resources, and improve the welfare of Bangsamoro farmers and fisherfolk.

Mandate

MAFAR is the BARMM ministry responsible for agriculture, fisheries, and agrarian reform in the region — including the functions transferred from the national Department of Agrarian Reform (DAR) in 2019, apart from adjudicatory functions.

The seal features a star, rice stalks, and stylized water/fish elements on a blue-and-green field — commonly used to represent agriculture, fisheries, and the guidance of the ministry's work, framed by the ministry's full name and "BARMM."

What This Portal Does

Client Registry

Maintain records for farmers, fisherfolk, and agrarian reform beneficiaries (ARBs), including contact details, location, and key sector information.

Programs Management

Track sectoral and cross-cutting programs, their funding sources, coverage areas, linked projects, and current status.

Project Tracking

Link individual projects to their parent programs, and monitor budgets, target beneficiaries, and timelines.

Secure Staff Access

Only registered staff accounts can view or edit records, protected by a login system with encrypted passwords and role-based permissions.

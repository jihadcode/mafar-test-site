Programs

MAFAR's flagship programs and sectoral programs tracked by MAFAR-LDS. This page gives the public a general overview — the full, up-to-date registry of programs (funding sources, coverage areas, linked projects, and status) is maintained by staff in the secure dashboard.

MAFAR Flagship Programs

BInHI

MAFAR's flagship program for agriculture, covering both Banner Programs and Component Programs:

Banner Programs
  • Rice Program
  • Corn Program
  • High Value Crops Development Program
  • Organic Agriculture Program
  • Halal Food Industry Development
  • Livestock
Component Programs
  • Agribusiness and Marketing Assistance Service
  • Involvement in the Agricultural and Fishery Councils

LUPA

MAFAR's flagship program for agrarian reform:

  • Land Tenure & Improvement
  • Agrarian Justice Delivery
  • Program Beneficiaries Development
  • Technical Advisory Support
  • ARCCESS
  • The Agrarian Reform Communities Project II (ARCP II)

ISDA

MAFAR's flagship program for fisheries development, supporting fisherfolk across BARMM's coastal and inland waters through sustainable fisheries and aquaculture initiatives. For the current, complete component list, see the official MAFAR website.

Programs Tracked by MAFAR-LDS

Support to Women ARBs (SWARB)

A province-wide support program for women agrarian reform beneficiaries, funded through the Gender and Development (GAD) Fund. Provides livelihood assistance and capacity-building activities.

Farm Field School – Rice-Based Farming

Hands-on farmer training covering 8 municipalities, funded by the National Budget. Teaches rice-based farming techniques directly in the field alongside enrolled farmers.

PalayCheck System — School on Air

A radio-based extension program teaching the PalayCheck rice production system province-wide, making agricultural training accessible even in areas without easy travel to in-person sessions.

Philippine Coffee Quality Competition 2026

A regional competition promoting coffee quality and coffee farmers across the province, funded through the BARMM Special Fund.

MAFAR & Bangsamoro Officials

A quick reference for MAFAR and regional leadership. Because BARMM is currently in a transition period ahead of its first regular parliamentary election, please treat this page as a general reference and confirm current details on the official Bangsamoro Government website linked at the bottom of each section.

Abunawas Maslamama
Minister, Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, and Agrarian Reform (MAFAR)

Minister Maslamama leads MAFAR's work supporting Bangsamoro farmers, fisherfolk, and agrarian reform beneficiaries, including sustainable agribusiness, fisheries development, and inclusive market-access initiatives across the region. He succeeded Mohammad Yacob, who moved on to become BARMM Senior Minister and later Speaker of the Bangsamoro Parliament.

For the ministry's latest news and the Minister's official messages, see the official MAFAR website.

Provincial & Field Coordination
Illustrative structure

MAFAR delivers services through provincial and field offices across BARMM's provinces — including MAFAR-Lanao del Sur, which this portal supports. Each provincial office typically coordinates agriculture, fisheries, and agrarian reform services for its area with MAFAR's central office.

This portal's own staff directory (next tab) reflects MAFAR-LDS's actual registered personnel. For a full region-wide list of provincial officials, see the official MAFAR website.

Abdulraof "Sammy Gambar" Macacua
Interim Chief Minister, Bangsamoro Autonomous Region in Muslim Mindanao

Macacua leads the Bangsamoro Transition Authority, the interim regional government, after being appointed to the post following the resignation of the previous Chief Minister, Ahod "Al Haj Murad" Ebrahim. As Chief Minister, he heads the regional Cabinet and presented the Bangsamoro Government's 2026 regional budget proposal to the Bangsamoro Parliament.

Hatimil E. Hassan
Deputy Chief Minister – Island Provinces

Assists the Chief Minister with governance and coordination for BARMM's island provinces (Basilan, Sulu, and Tawi-Tawi).

This office is currently held on an interim basis. For the latest, see the official Chief Minister's Corner.

The Bangsamoro Transition Authority Parliament
80 interim members, presidentially appointed

Until the region's first regular parliamentary election, the Bangsamoro Transition Authority exercises legislative power through an 80-member interim Parliament. Its members are currently appointed rather than elected.

First Regular Parliamentary Election
Scheduled for September 2026

Under Republic Act No. 12317, BARMM's first regular parliamentary election has been set for the second Monday of September 2026. Once elected, the 80-seat Parliament will be composed of 50% regional political party representatives, 40% district representatives, and 10% sectoral representatives, serving until June 30, 2031.

Because membership will change with the 2026 election, this page does not list individual MPs. For the current roster, see the official BARMM Officials directory.
